Arklogs is a personal archive for things I build and things I learn. It will hold project logs, ESP32 experiments, security research, CTF writeups, and smaller observations along the way.

I want the site to feel made by hand. Its visual pieces are a starting frame for my own drawings, and the writing will make room for both completed work and the process behind it.

More entries will appear as they are ready to share.
